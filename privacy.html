Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Tanjore (OrderBook) is an order and inventory management app.

What the app accesses

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app requests access to your Google Drive. It uses this access only to upload, download, and sync your own order data and photos between your devices.

How data is used and stored

Your data is stored on your device and in your own Google Drive account. We do not run any server that collects or stores your Google data. We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can delete synced files directly from your Google Drive.
